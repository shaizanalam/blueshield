# BlueShield RO Industries

## Website Redesign — Master Design & Product Specification

**Project:** BlueShield RO Industries Private Limited
**Website:** blueshieldro.net
**Location:** Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India
**Business Type:** Water-treatment equipment manufacturer, supplier and installation provider
**Primary Market:** B2B / Industrial / Commercial / Institutional
**Design Goal:** Transform the existing catalog-style website into a premium, modern, trustworthy water-engineering company website.

---

# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

BlueShield RO Industries Private Limited is a Raipur-based manufacturer and supplier operating in the water-treatment industry.

The company started in 2016 and offers water-treatment equipment including:

* RO Plants
* Industrial RO Plants
* Commercial RO Systems
* Water Purifiers
* Water Softeners
* Water Treatment Plants
* Mineral Water Plants
* Domestic RO Spare Parts
* Industrial RO Spare Parts
* Swimming Pool Filtration
* Swimming Pool Services
* Water Bottling Equipment
* Water Filling Machines
* Water Pouch Packing Machines
* Ozone Generator Systems
* DM Water Plants
* DEF Plants
* STP Plants
* Filtration Systems
* Related water-treatment equipment

The company also provides installation services.

The current website behaves primarily like a product-directory/catalog website. The redesign should turn it into a **credible engineering brand + product catalog + lead-generation platform**.

---

# 2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The new website should accomplish five things:

1. Establish BlueShield as a professional water-treatment engineering company.
2. Make products extremely easy to discover.
3. Explain technical products without overwhelming non-technical customers.
4. Generate high-quality quotation/inquiry leads.
5. Build trust before the customer contacts the company.

The website should feel like:

**Engineering + Purity + Reliability + Industrial Capability**

Not:

**Generic marketplace / old-style product listing website**
